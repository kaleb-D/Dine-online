About Us

Our Mission: Quality and Speed.
Dine Online was founded in Addis Ababa to bridge the gap between high-quality restaurant food and fast, reliable delivery. We partner only with the best local kitchens to bring authentic Ethiopian and international cuisine straight to your door.

We are committed to exceptional service, fresh ingredients, and ensuring every order arrives hot and on time. We are more than just delivery; we are your local dining solution.
